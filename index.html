⚙️ Automation Kya Hai?

Automation matlab tumhara digital mulazim — customer ka message aaya, foran jawab gaya. Booking hui, reminder gaya, follow-up gaya. Tum so rahe ho, tumhara business chal raha hai. Na salary mangta hai, na chhutti leta hai, na bhoolta hai.

🌐 Website Kyun Zaroori Hai?

Tumhari dukaan raat ko band hoti hai — website kabhi nahi. Customer Google par dhoondta hai, website par bharosa karta hai, aur order karta hai. Professional image = zyada customers = zyada paisa. Hum fast, modern websites banate hain — jo sirf dikhti nahi, bechti hain.
